Oatle Kids
Little Adventures. Big Discoveries.

Oatle Kids is a space-themed educational web app for children aged 2–7, developed by Oatle Technologies. It combines early literacy, puzzles, creative play, stories, and interactive activities to help young learners practise foundational skills in an encouraging environment.

The project began as Atli's Space Game, a solar-system reading adventure created to support learning at home. Feedback from an initial beta with 18 participating families helped shape its expansion into a broader early-learning experience.
Learning experiences
Ages 2–3: Early learning through play
The younger learners' section is designed around short, visual, touch-friendly activities:
- Balloon Adventures — early recognition and visual interaction
- Puzzle Adventures — matching and simple problem-solving
- Building Adventures — stacking, arranging, and exploring blocks
- Animal Adventures — learning about animals through interactive play
- Music & Movement — listening, rhythm, and movement activities
These categories are being developed and expanded; not every activity is publicly available yet.
Ages 4–7: Building skills through adventure
Learning areas include:
- Reading Adventures — letters, phonics, words, and reading
- Words & Sentences — word completion and sentence construction
- Numbers & Shapes — introductory numeracy and shape recognition
- Writing & Tracing — handwriting and letter-formation practice
- Thinking & Problem Solving — puzzles, logic, and creative challenges
- Reading & Stories — illustrated stories and comprehension
Cosmic Puzzles adds picture-based challenges to the older children's experience. Additional activities, including space-themed building games and original stories, are in development.
Atli's Space Reading Adventure
The original literacy journey takes children through the solar system. Each planet focuses on a different reading skill.
Planet	Learning skill
Mercury	Capital letters
Venus	Lowercase letters
Earth	Uppercase and lowercase matching
Mars	Letter sounds and phonics
Jupiter	Reading simple words
Saturn	Missing letters
Uranus	Building simple sentences
Neptune	Reading an illustrated children's story
Pluto	End-of-journey literacy assessment


Children practise letter recognition, phonics, word formation, sentence building, story reading, and recalling what they have learned.
Game experience
Oatle Kids uses:
- Colourful space-themed illustrations and age-appropriate challenges
- Interactive questions, puzzles, and hands-on activities
- Audio guidance and encouraging feedback
- Short activities, repetition, and opportunities to try again
- Touch-friendly controls for phones and tablets
The approach is non-punitive: mistakes are opportunities to practise, not reasons to penalise children.
For parents and caregivers
The original Pluto assessment summarises a child's performance across the literacy skills introduced in the planet journey. It is an informational learning snapshot, not a formal reading-level assessment, developmental evaluation, or diagnosis.

The app is designed to support learning at home, including homeschooling families.
Beta testing
The first beta programme included 18 participating families. Their feedback informed changes to navigation, usability, activities, and age-appropriate learning experiences. Further content development and testing are ongoing.
Technology
The project uses:
- React and Vite
- JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS
- Supabase for application data and authentication
- Browser storage for supported local state
- Web Audio and audio assets
- Progressive Web App (PWA) capabilities
- Vercel for web deployment
Access and distribution
Official website: https://www.oatlekids.co.za/

Oatle Kids is being prepared for a web/PWA-first release. On supported devices, the web app can be added to the home screen for convenient access without an app-store download.

Google Play distribution is planned next, with the Apple App Store to follow. Neither store release should be assumed available until officially announced. Full learning-app access may remain restricted during pre-launch preparation.
Project status
Active development / pre-launch — October 2026

The original nine-planet literacy experience, illustrated reading journey, and final assessment formed the first completed game version. The expanded Oatle Kids platform is still being developed and prepared for broader release.

Current priorities include:
- Expanding games and age-specific learning content
- Developing more original stories and creative activities
- Refining puzzles, building, music, and movement experiences
- Improving parent onboarding and account access
- Completing subscription and promotional-code functionality
- Security, reliability, testing, and app-store readiness
Feature availability and release timing may change during development.
