FROM ONE PARENT TO ANOTHER

Before I built Oatle Kids,
I was teaching my own.

I’m Ntombi, a homeschooling parent and the CEO of Oatle Technologies, a software development agency. But this project started with my children, long before it became something I could share with other families.

I’ve been teaching my children since they were born. As they’ve grown, I’ve helped them recognise letters, build words, put sentences together and make sense of numbers. These are some of the foundations I’ve been working on with them to prepare them for Grade 1.

Oatle Kids brings those ways of learning into an app. It’s my way of sharing what I’ve been doing with my own children and giving other parents a place to start teaching theirs.

I want you to feel that you can be part of your child’s learning. Sit beside them, talk through a word, help them build a sentence, and give them time to try again. The app gives you activities to explore; the time you spend together is yours.

Ntombi
Homeschooling parent & founder of Oatle Kids

KEEP THE ADVENTURES CLOSE

Install Oatle Kids

Add Oatle Kids to your home screen or computer, then open it from its icon. You can also keep playing in your browser.

iPhone or iPad

Open this website in Safari. Tap Share (you may find it in the page menu), then Add to Home Screen. If Open as Web App appears, turn it on, then tap Add.

If Add to Home Screen is missing, scroll to Edit Actions in the Share menu and add it.

Android: Chrome or Samsung Internet

Open this website in Chrome or Samsung Internet. Open the browser menu and look for Install app, Add to Home screen, or Add page to → Home screen. Follow the instructions to add Oatle Kids.

An install icon may also appear beside the address bar. You do not need to wait for a pop-up.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon beside the address bar or the install option in the browser menu. In Safari on a recent Mac, choose File → Add to Dock.

Recent Firefox versions on Windows offer a web app button beside the address bar. Firefox on Mac and Linux does not currently offer this option; use Chrome, Edge, or supported Safari instead.

No install option?

If you opened this link inside WhatsApp, Facebook, or another app, use its menu to open it in Safari, Chrome, or Samsung Internet. Use a normal browsing window and an up-to-date browser.

On Firefox for Android, look in the menu for Install or Add to Home screen; the result may be a shortcut. If Oatle Kids is already installed, open its existing icon. You can always open Oatle Kids in your browser.

Internet is needed to sign in and manage your subscription. Adding the icon does not create an account or start a subscription.

A FEW THINGS PARENTS ASK

Before you blast off.

Something else on your mind?

Who is Oatle Kids for?

The app has Little Explorers activities for ages 2–3 and learning adventures for ages 4–7. These are starting points; choose activities that suit your child and support them as they explore.

What device can we use?

Use a modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Log in through the button at the top of this page. Dedicated mobile apps are coming soon.

Do we need an internet connection?

You need internet to create an account, sign in and set up access. The app supports offline caching after loading, but keep a connection available for account and payment features.

Is this a school or a formal assessment?

No. Oatle Kids offers activities for practising early learning skills with a parent or caregiver. It does not replace teaching, professional assessment or individual learning support.

How do access and payments work?

Subscriptions open on 16 November. Choose Single Child at R120 per month or Family at R200 per month. Promo codes grant Single Child access only. Use Log in to open your parent account and manage access to the adventures. For help, email support@oatlekids.co.za.
