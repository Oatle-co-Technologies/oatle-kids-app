Karaoke music credits

These are original instrumental arrangements and digital performances created for Oatle Kids. Melody, gentle accompaniment and count-in are synthesized directly from note data. No external recordings, sound fonts, sampled instruments or singer recordings are included.

The songs use traditional public-domain melodies. The references below document the traditional tunes; historical versions can differ from the familiar versions used here. The text follows traditional English nursery verses. The original instrumentals and their cue timings were created for this app.

ABC and Twinkle share the traditional French melody Ah! vous dirai-je, maman. Are You Sleeping? uses the traditional Frère Jacques melody. The historical Crane collections were published in the nineteenth century; Walter Crane lived 1845–1915 and Lucy Crane 1842–1882. The referenced Row Your Boat and Frère Jacques notation is released under CC0 by Mysid.
