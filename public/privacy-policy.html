Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Oatle Kids handles personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Responsible party: Ntombizodwa Moekwa, trading as Oatle Technologies

Country: South Africa

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Privacy contact: info@oatle-technologies.co.za

Child profiles and progress

We store children’s nicknames, optional profile details and saved learning progress in the parent account so profiles can be kept separate and progress can be restored on another registered device. Progress is also cached locally. We keep device labels, random device identifiers, session identifiers and recent activity times to enforce the plan’s device and simultaneous-play limits. Do not put a child’s full name or sensitive information in a nickname or saved artwork. Removing a device ends its play access without deleting the child’s cloud progress. Contact info@oatle-technologies.co.za for account or child-data deletion requests.

1. Information we collect

  • Parent account information, such as an email address and authentication records.
  • Subscription and access status, payment references and transaction status. Full card details are entered with PayFast and are not stored by Oatle Kids.
  • Technical information needed for security and reliability, such as browser type, IP address and service logs.
  • Preferences and activity progress stored on the device where applicable.
  • Questions voluntarily submitted to the Oatle Kids AI assistant. Parents must not submit passwords, payment details or identifying information about a child.

2. Children’s privacy

Oatle Kids is purchased and managed by a parent or adult caregiver. Children do not create their own login accounts. A parent can create a profile using a nickname and optional learning details. Please avoid full names, contact details or other identifying information about a child. Parents should supervise use and contact us if they believe a child’s personal information was submitted.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, confirm subscription access, provide and improve activities, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, meet legal obligations and communicate essential service information.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to operate the service, including Supabase for account and access services, Vercel for website hosting, PayFast for payment processing and Cloudflare for the AI assistant. Each provider processes information for its service and under its own privacy and security obligations.

5. Legal grounds and sharing

Information is processed where needed to provide the service, comply with law, protect legitimate security interests or act with consent. We do not sell personal information. We share only what is necessary with service providers, regulators or law enforcement when legally required, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

6. Storage and security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards and retain information only for as long as needed for service, legal, accounting and dispute purposes. No internet system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where relevant, or complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator. Email us from the account address so we can verify the request.

8. Cookies and local storage

Oatle Kids uses essential browser storage for login, security, installation, preferences and activity progress. Disabling essential storage may prevent the application from working correctly.

9. Contact and changes

Contact info@oatle-technologies.co.za with privacy questions. Material policy changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
